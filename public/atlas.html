CSE1006 Indian Knowledge Systems

Chronology of Indian Science, Math & Metallurgy

~800 BCE

Baudhayana Sulba Sutras (Geometry)

Formulated geometric rules for altar construction and documented the relationship $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$ centuries before Pythagoras, alongside fractional rational approximations of $\sqrt{2}$.

~4th Century BCE

Panini's Ashtadhyayi (Generative Linguistics)

Designed a formal rule-based generative grammar of 4,000 algorithmic sutras. Recognized by modern computer scientists as the foundational forerunner to Backus-Naur Form (BNF) in compiler theory.

~4th Century CE

Delhi Iron Pillar (Corrosion-Resistant Metallurgy)

A 6-tonne forged wrought iron pillar standing exposed to monsoon rains for over 1,600 years without rusting due to a high-phosphorus catalytic protective film ($\delta$-FeOOH / misawite).

499 CE

Aryabhatiya by Aryabhata (Astronomy & Zero)

Formalized the decimal place-value system, introduced the concept of zero (*Shunya*), stated Earth rotates on its axis, and computed $\pi \approx 3.1416$.

~12th Century CE

Wootz Crucible Steel & Zawar Zinc Smelting

Southern Indian high-carbon crucible steel (*Ukku*) exported worldwide for legendary Damascus swords, while Zawar in Rajasthan operated the world's earliest industrial downward-distillation zinc smelting furnaces.
